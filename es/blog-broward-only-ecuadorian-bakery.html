Broward's only Ecuadorian bakery: D'Ambato Bakery & Pastries in Cooper CityLa única panadería ecuatoriana de Broward: D'Ambato Bakery & Pastries en Cooper City

October 27, 2026 · D'Ambato at Flamingo Supermarket, Cooper City27 de octubre de 2026 · D'Ambato en Flamingo Supermarket, Cooper City

Broward County has Cuban bakeries, Colombian bakeries, Venezuelan bakeries, Argentine bakeries — and until Flamingo Supermarket opened in Cooper City, not one Ecuadorian bakery. Ecuadorians in Pembroke Pines, Davie, Weston and Hollywood drove to Miami for a restaurant meal and baked their own bread at home. D'Ambato Bakery & Pastries, inside Flamingo at 2581 N Hiatus Rd, is Broward's only Ecuadorian bakery, and the only Ecuadorian bakery in South Florida with bread baked fresh every day.

Why "Ambato"?

Ambato sits in the Ecuadorian highlands under the Tungurahua volcano, and it is the country's bread city. Ask any Ecuadorian where the best pan comes from and the answer is Ambato: dense, slightly sweet loaves and rolls that families buy warm by the bag. The name D'Ambato — de Ambato, "from Ambato" — is a promise about the style of bread, not just a place on a map. The logo says the rest: the volcano, the wheat, the loaves.

What's baked every day

  • Pan de yuca — warm cheese bread, chewy inside, the thing customers buy by the dozen.
  • Humitas and empanadas — sweet corn tamales in the husk and baked empanadas, fresh each morning.
  • Ecuadorian breads and pan dulce — enrollados, bizcochos, sweet rolls for coffee.
  • Tres leches, mousse cakes, Biscoff cake, pastelitos de guayaba — by the slice or whole.
  • Custom cakes for birthdays, quinces and holidays — order 48 hours ahead at the counter or at (954) 228-0435.
  • Guaguas de pan and colada morada — every October and the first week of November for Día de los Difuntos. Read about the tradition →

Why "fresh daily" matters

Most Latin bakeries in Broward bake a few times a week and sell from the case the rest of the time; some bring product in frozen. At D'Ambato the counter is restocked from the oven every day the store is open — seven days, 7 AM to 10 PM — so the pan de yuca you buy on a Tuesday afternoon was made that Tuesday. If you want the first batch, come early: bread is on the counter from opening.

Make it a trip

D'Ambato is one of six kitchens inside Flamingo Supermarket. Grab a coffee and pan de yuca at the bakery, then an encebollado or bolón from La Casita de los Ñaños next door, and do the week's shopping while you're here — the aisles carry Ecuadorian brands, a kosher section and products from 17+ countries. We're in the Embassy Lakes Shopping Center at Hiatus Rd and Sheridan St, about 10 minutes from Pembroke Pines and Davie and 15 from Weston. The mailing address says Hollywood, FL 33026; the store is in Cooper City.

Quick answers

Is there an Ecuadorian bakery in Broward County? Yes — D'Ambato Bakery & Pastries inside Flamingo Supermarket, Cooper City.
Hours? Every day 7 AM – 10 PM.
Can I order a cake? Yes, 48 hours' notice; call (954) 228-0435.
When is colada morada available? Mid-October through about November 7.

¿Por qué "Ambato"?

Qué se hornea cada día

  • Pan de yuca: pan de queso caliente, suave por dentro, el que la gente compra por docena.
  • Humitas y empanadas: humitas de choclo en su hoja y empanadas al horno, frescas cada mañana.
  • Panes ecuatorianos y pan dulce: enrollados, bizcochos, panes dulces para el café.
  • Tres leches, tortas de mousse, torta Biscoff, pastelitos de guayaba: por porción o enteras.

Por qué importa "fresco todos los días"

La mayoría de las panaderías latinas de Broward hornean unas pocas veces por semana y venden de la vitrina el resto del tiempo; algunas traen producto congelado. En D'Ambato el mostrador se repone del horno cada día que abre la tienda, los siete días, de 7 AM a 10 PM, así que el pan de yuca que compras un martes por la tarde se hizo ese martes. Si quieres la primera tanda, ven temprano: hay pan en el mostrador desde la apertura.
