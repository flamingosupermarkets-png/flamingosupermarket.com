Questions

When and where do I pick up?

Thanksgiving Day, Thursday Nov 26, between 10 AM and 5 PM at Flamingo Supermarket, 2581 N Hiatus Rd, Cooper City (Embassy Lakes Shopping Center). Bring your order confirmation.

How does the 50% deposit work?

When you choose a size, pick “50% deposit” and you pay half online. You pay the other half at pickup.

How do the by-the-pound items work?

We weigh them at pickup. If that’s all you’re ordering, pay a $50 deposit online. If your order already has $50 or more of other food, choose “Pay at pickup”.

What’s the last day to order?

Wednesday Nov 25 at 8 PM, or earlier if we sell out. If online ordering is closed, call us at (954) 228-0435 and we’ll see what we can do.

Can I order something that isn’t on the list?

Call or text us at (954) 228-0435. For big parties we can put together a custom package.
